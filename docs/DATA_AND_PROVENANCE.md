# Data and provenance

The saved evidence and manuscript are copied from the verified September 25 r3
release. Scientific result tables, fits, protocol locks, correction receipts,
failed-study decisions and statistical audit reports are preserved byte-for-byte.
Additional research code and tests are a development snapshot, not a claim that
every file existed when the experimental locks were created.

The corrected primary and first multi-city studies are post-hoc replays, not
new untouched holdouts. The separate external studies retain their own recorded
lineages. No fitting, allocation replay or paper-level hypothesis test was
performed while preparing this export.

## Privacy and source terms

The 529 raw ballot files recorded in `EXCLUDED_RAW_DATA.json` are omitted.
Their bytes were not rechecked in the data-free verification. Other local raw
data and behavioral-pilot material are also excluded.

Authenticated normalization diagnostics retain one public-source ballot excerpt,
repeated in receipts, an audit and source constants. It contains the source-file
identifier, district/year, numeric row identifier/index and project IDs, but no
names, addresses, contact details or demographics. Review that limited excerpt
and applicable data terms before public distribution.

The upstream Pabulib snapshot is recorded at commit
`2f4321fec84069f50abf35fb5e90852013d17070`. See `tempopb/PABULIB_SOURCE.md`
and the corpus manifests for exact inputs and hashes. This export does not
establish a raw-data redistribution license.

## Export omissions

The repository omits embedded historical release archives, outdated PDFs,
editorial/humanization tests, agent instructions, review drafts, iteration
reports, scratch plans, caches, environments, credentials and unrelated
behavioral/ICAIF tooling. Scientific analysis files containing words such as
"audit" or "reviewer" are retained when they implement or document the research.

The original sealed r3 supplement remains unchanged outside this export.
