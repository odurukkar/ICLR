# Attribution and licensing

No project-wide software license was present in the source workspace. This
export does not invent one or grant rights on the authors' behalf. The authors
should select an appropriate license before a public release.

Existing copyright and license notices in the bundled LaTeX style files are
preserved. Those notices govern their respective files, not the whole project.

Election inputs originate from Pabulib. Source filenames, upstream revision and
digests are documented in `tempopb/PABULIB_SOURCE.md` and the saved manifests.
Raw ballot files are not redistributed because permission was not established.

The three main figures use the visual style of Gorishniy, Kotelnikov and Babenko,
**TabM**, ICLR 2025, as a reference. Their numerical data are this project's own
stored results; the reference paper's data and figure images are not included.

Reference: https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/c1ba41c694834aeef91ae161711d4939-Paper-Conference.pdf
